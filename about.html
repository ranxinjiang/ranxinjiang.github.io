01 — My Background

How I Got Here

I'm a first-generation college student pursuing a Master's degree in Information Systems with a concentration in Data Analytics at Baruch College's Zicklin School of Business, after graduating summa cum laude from the University at Buffalo with a Bachelor's in Information Technology & Management.

Across my academic and professional experiences, I've worked on projects involving data analysis, systems implementation, and technology-driven process improvement — leading the migration of over 100 LMS training courses at MJHS Health System, analyzing platform and curriculum gaps at Houghton Mifflin Harcourt, running production operations for a six-week film project at Prologue Films, and supporting process improvement work at the NYC Mayor's Office of Management & Budget.

I've always been drawn to opportunities that let me learn by doing, and to projects where I can see the real-world impact of my work. I like environments where I'm working closely with other people to figure out how something can be improved — organizing information more clearly, streamlining a process, or supporting the rollout of a new system.

What I keep coming back to is being involved across the full lifecycle of a project, from planning through execution, where I can take an active role in turning an idea into something people actually use.
