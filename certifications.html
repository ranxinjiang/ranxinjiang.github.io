Corporate Analyst Development Program (CADP) Job Simulation

Completed October 2023

The problem

A five-module simulation of the day-to-day work of a CADP analyst, built around a home mortgage business line — finding revenue opportunities in customer data, then improving the process that serves them.

What I did

  • Cleaned a home mortgage dataset (duplicates, missing values) and analyzed it in Excel with pivot tables to identify high-potential sales prospects
  • Built a data visualization dashboard summarizing the prospect analysis
  • Mapped the mortgage loan underwriting and origination process using UML process-map conventions, then identified improvement opportunities
  • Built a PowerPoint narrative around one improvement opportunity, arguing the case for automation on turnaround time and operational cost
  • Recorded a stakeholder project update video as the team lead

Delivered

An Excel analysis with charts, a visualization dashboard, a one-page process map with improvement recommendations, a stakeholder-facing deck, and a recorded project update.
