Cloud · Data engineering

Streaming Finance Data Pipeline

Graduate Project · Big Data Technologies · Apr – May 2026

A near real-time pipeline built end to end on AWS. A Lambda producer pulls daily prices for 66 companies from the Alpha Vantage API and streams each record into Kinesis; Firehose delivers them to partitioned S3 storage; Glue catalogs the schema; Athena queries the raw files in place. The point was infrastructure that handles data as it arrives rather than in a nightly batch.

R2E Tenant Management Database

Graduate Project · Database Management · Nov – Dec 2025

R2E, LLC is a fictional property management company created for a graduate database management course. The scenario: several residential buildings, their units and tenants, an in-house maintenance team, and a growing pile of Excel spreadsheets that had stopped being readable. The build: a relational Access database with four data-entry forms behind a central switchboard, four management reports, and four analytical queries covering open work orders, completion times, and employee workload.
